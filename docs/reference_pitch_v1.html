04 / quality system

Trust is the product layer.

No “zero-defect” promise. The value is a defined acceptance process, supplier qualification and traceable documentation.

01 / SUPPLIER

Capability first.

Machine list, material capability, achievable tolerances, lead time, sample quality and process fit are recorded before assignment.

02 / INSPECTION

Evidence, not adjectives.

Dimensional checks, visual inspection and photo documentation are tied to the agreed acceptance criteria.

03 / HISTORY

Every order teaches the next.

Supplier performance becomes structured data: on-time delivery, quality events, process strengths and repeat-order history.

04 / WHY BUYERS STAY

More evidence.
